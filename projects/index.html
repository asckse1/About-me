Monument Of Souls

If you're more into video games than tabletop games, you're probably here for this one. this project is still ongoing, sitting at roughly 3% completion. monument of souls is a tower climbing VR-RPG intended to bring the action and fun from media like The Max Level Player of Tutorial Tower and Sword Art Online to the real world. Complete with Generative AI powered NPCs (Have you ever had an NPC realistically beg for its life before? no? good, dont. its deeply unsettling), and procedural side quests generation. the main scenario has the player climbing the titular monument in their quest to godhood, making friends and growing their skills along the way. Intended Feature Set: - Full KatwalkVR support - Haptic Vest and Glove support (A brand poll will be posted on socials) - Generated changes to the world environment based on sidequest progress and content (Work in progress. currently using a combination of GenAI and a procedural set of changes, though in the future i intend to shift to microsoft's potential 3d model generator - if it comes out) - Class Advancement Quests - completely overhaul traditional class advancement systems by analyzing player input data and designing a build from a list of potential skills based on their playstyle.
